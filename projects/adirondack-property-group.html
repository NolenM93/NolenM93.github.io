Adirondack Property Group

A booking website for a local rental company in the Adirondacks. Real availability, real reservations, and a flow that customers can actually use, without calling a phone number or guessing at dates.

The problem

A local property rental business in the Adirondack region needed a way for customers to book rentals online. The existing process (phone calls, emails, back-and-forth about dates) was not scaling. Customers wanted to see what was available and book it themselves.

The solution

A custom-built booking website that shows availability, handles reservations, and lets the owner manage properties from a dashboard. Live Stripe checkout and auto-locking calendars are out of scope for the current signed plan.

Availability calendar

Real dates, real availability. No guessing, no phone tag.

Online booking

Customers complete the reservation inquiry process online.

Property pages

One page per listing, with photo galleries and location maps.

Mobile-first

Works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
