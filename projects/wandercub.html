Personal project

WanderCub

A mobile app for families that delivers short, self-contained guided micro-adventures for kids aged 5 to 12. Nature spotting, observation challenges, and scavenger hunts designed for lakes, parks, towns, and outdoor destinations.

DartFlutterOffline-first
Back to work

Product idea

Each episode includes a short narrative intro, 3 to 6 structured real-world tasks, a clear completion condition, and an ending screen. Core gameplay is meant to work without internet after the initial download. Parents start an episode; kids complete the tasks.

There is no public app-store listing yet. The source, bundled episodes, and Flutter tests live in the GitHub repo.
