FDA Recall Risk Monitor

A Python and Streamlit web app that turns openFDA food enforcement reports into a searchable, filterable dashboard with risk scoring, charts, and a US choropleth of recall volume.

PythonStreamlitPandasPlotly
GitHub repoBack to repos

Features in the source include keyword search, classification and date filters, CSV export, summary metric cards, a monthly trend chart, and regex extraction of US state codes from the distribution pattern field.
