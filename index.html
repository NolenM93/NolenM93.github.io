What happens after launch

A cheap one-time site often leaves you with a template you cannot edit, a domain that is not in your name, and no one to call when something breaks. That is the part we want to be clear about up front.

With this plan, we are still here after the site or app goes live. Hosting, updates, and backups are part of the monthly rate. You get unlimited edits for the first 30 days after launch (changes to what we already built, not new features or extra pages beyond the plan). After that, a set number of edit hours are included each month. Your domain, your content, and your payment, Google, Apple, or Play accounts stay in your name. If you want to leave after the term, you keep a copy.

FAQ

Common questions

How does the free consultation work?

Send a note about your business and what you need. We will reply to set up a short call. We talk through your goals, recommend a plan or a custom quote, and there is no charge for that conversation. If it is not a fit, we will say so.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the plan and on how quickly content, photos, and feedback come back. We will give you a timeline before work starts.

How do you use AI?

We design, review, and own the code. AI agents help with faster builds, automated testing, and security checks.

How do I get started?

Email us. We have the consult, pick a plan, and send a short agreement and the monthly payment details. After those are in place, the build starts. You will have a clear point of contact from that first email through launch and the months after.

What edits are included?

Every plan includes unlimited edits for the first 30 days after launch. That covers changes to the site or app we already agreed to build, such as copy, photos, hours, and layout tweaks. It does not include new features or new pages beyond your plan. Those are quoted first. After day 30, Tier 1 includes 3 hours of edits each month, Tier 2 includes 6 hours, and Tier 3 includes 8 hours. Unused hours do not roll over.

What if I cancel?

Plans have a 12-month minimum term. After that, you can cancel with 30 days' written notice. If you leave during the first year, you pay the remaining months of that term, and then you get a copy of the site or app. After 12 paid months, that copy transfers at no extra buyout fee. Your content and your domain stay yours either way. The exact terms are in the service agreement.

What do I pay Apple or Google for a mobile app?

You pay Apple and Google directly: $99 per year for the Apple Developer Program, and a one-time $25 fee for a Google Play developer account. Those accounts stay in your name. Any store commissions on paid apps or in-app purchases are yours too. They are not part of the monthly plan.
